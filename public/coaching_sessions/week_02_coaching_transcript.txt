ASSISTANT
The time element distinguishes machine-readable datetime from human-readable display. A date like 'Mon 09 Nov 2026' is not the standard date format expected in datetime. Return two fields:
SELECT title, description, location,
  TO_CHAR(date, 'YYYY-MM-DD') AS date_iso,
  TO_CHAR(date, 'Dy DD Mon YYYY') AS date_display

Then render:

This produces a standardized datetime value and readable display text.
